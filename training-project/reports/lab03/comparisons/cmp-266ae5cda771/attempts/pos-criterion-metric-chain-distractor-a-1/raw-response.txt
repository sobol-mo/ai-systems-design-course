  "proposed_text": "In evaluation design, a criterion specifies the quality property that a system must satisfy, serving as the target condition or standard against which performance is judged, while a metric is a concrete, defined measurement that quantifies an aspect of the system's behavior; by comparing the observed metric values to the criterion—such as checking whether a metric exceeds a threshold or falls within an acceptable range—we determine whether the system meets the desired property, thereby linking the abstract requirement of the criterion to the empirical evidence provided by the metric."
